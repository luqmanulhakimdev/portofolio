01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Engineering with care.

I’ve spent 9+ years developing web applications and backend systems, working across projects and with teams of different sizes.

Over the past nine years, my engineering journey has centered around building dependable backend architectures, payment gateways, and transaction-intensive platforms. I treat software engineering as the art of making complex systems predictable—crafting code that gracefully handles high throughput, intermittent third-party outages, and network retries while upholding strict data integrity and consistency.

I have a deep appreciation for clean, layered architecture and operational observability. Experience has taught me that software is maintained far longer than it is built; thus, clear separation of concerns, structured error handling, and end-to-end metrics make all the difference. From designing microservices in Go and Node.js to monitoring with Grafana, Prometheus, and Dead Letter Queues, I ensure systems are transparent and resilient before problems reach users.

Beyond code, I believe exceptional engineering is fundamentally a collaborative craft. I thrive when translating nuanced business requirements into elegant API contracts alongside product managers and cross-functional teams. I value clear technical documentation, constructive code reviews, and cultivating sustainable engineering practices that help teams build and scale reliable software with confidence.
